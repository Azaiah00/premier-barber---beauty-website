07Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still wondering? Call or text the shop at (804) 332-7091

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, and several of our barbers (including Adonis and Saint) regularly take them. Appointments are preferred and guarantee your spot, so book online if you’re on a schedule.

How do I book?

Every pro at Premier keeps their own book. Tap “Book” on any pro’s card and you’ll go straight to their live calendar on Booksy, StyleSeat or theCut, where you pick a time and confirm in about a minute.

Where are you located, and is there parking?

We’re at 11800-G Hull Street Road in Midlothian, VA 23112, serving Richmond, Chesterfield and the surrounding area. There’s easy parking in the lot right outside.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Absolutely. Our barbers cut kids as young as two, with kid, pre-teen and teen pricing. Parents often mention Darnell’s and Adonis’s patience with children on the autism spectrum.

Do you do braids, wigs and silk presses?

Yes. Samaya handles the beauty side: knotless, Fulani and stitch braids, wig installs and customization, quick weaves, silk presses, and loc retwists and starter locs.

What does a haircut cost?

Standard adult cuts at Premier run from $30 to $52.50 depending on the barber and service. Premium services, like Darnell’s Gold package or Adonis’s textured fringe, are $80. Kids’ cuts start at $20. See the full menu for every price.

How do payments and deposits work?

Cards are accepted. Each pro sets their own deposit and cancellation policy on their booking page. For example, Darnell requires a deposit and charges for late cancellations. Check your pro’s page before booking.

Is the shop hiring or renting booths?

We periodically open chairs for motivated barbers, stylists, locticians and braiders. Call or text (804) 332-7091 to ask about current availability.
