01About Maal

Maal is a master barber with more than 15 years behind the chair and a book that runs from first haircuts to 65-and-up regulars. His signature is a clean blend finished with the razor, done carefully and never rushed.

Clients stay for years. They talk about how carefully he works, how kind he is with kids and seniors, and how he checks every angle before you stand up. He also takes a limited number of mobile and after-hours appointments across the Richmond and Petersburg area.
