01About Darnell

Darnell learned the trade in Brooklyn, New York, and has been cutting for 28 years. He worked beside some of the city’s best barbers before bringing that standard to Richmond. He’s also a tattoo artist, which shows in how carefully he handles detail.

His chair is where people come for crisp line-ups, fades, tapers and afros, and for a barber who is patient with kids, including children on the autism spectrum. Clients describe him as professional, personable, on time and meticulous. His goal is simple: to make you look and feel good.
