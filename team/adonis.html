01About Adonis

Adonis works as “The Jack of All Fadez”: a versatile barber who can do nearly any fade, from temp fades and textured fringes to women’s cuts with designs and freehand art.

He opens early, welcomes walk-ins any time, and asks that you call or text so he knows you’re coming. Parents of children on the spectrum have singled him out for his time and patience.
