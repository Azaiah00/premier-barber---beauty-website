More than a cut.

Premier Barber & Beauty Salon is a home base for independent barbers and stylists in Midlothian, where precision barbering and beauty work share one address, one standard and one community.

{{ m.kicker('01', 'Our story') }}

Built by the chair.

Most shops make you choose: the barbershop for your line-up, a salon across town for braids, and somewhere else again for the kids. Premier was built so you don’t have to. Under one roof on Hull Street Road you’ll find master barbers with decades of combined experience next to a braid-and-wig specialist whose clients say nobody else can touch their hair.

Every pro here runs their own business: their own book, their own style, their own regulars. The standard is shared. The chair is clean, your time is respected, the cut is checked from every angle, and the conversation is good.

Clients range from two-year-olds getting their first cut to 65-and-up regulars. You’ll find parents of kids on the autism spectrum who finally found a barber with patience, and professionals who haven’t trusted anyone else in years. That mix is what the shop is about.

“I’ve worked beside a lot of great barbers in Brooklyn, and now I’m working in Richmond beside some more great barbers.”Darnell, OG barber, 28 years in the chair
